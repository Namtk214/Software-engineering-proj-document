\section{Software Requirements Specification}

\subsection{Introduction}

\subsubsection{Product Overview}

Francais.vn is a self-learning French platform for Vietnamese learners. The MVP provides a beginner-to-A1 learning path, micro-exercises, dictation practice, note-taking while studying, and later support for exam preparation. The product is mobile-first and web-only during the MVP stage.

\paragraph{Elevator Pitch.}
Francais.vn is a French self-study platform for Vietnamese users. It helps learners follow a clear learning path from the beginning to A1 level, practice through micro-exercises and dictation, take quick notes during lessons, and prepare for certificate-oriented learning after the MVP.

\paragraph{Vision.}
The long-term vision is to become the leading French self-learning platform for Vietnamese learners by combining structured learning paths, personalized progress, exam-oriented learning, and a lightweight note-taking experience.

Payment, AI grading, community features, native mobile applications, placement tests, full mock tests with timers, multi-admin RBAC, push notifications, and email drip campaigns are outside the MVP.

\subsection{User and System Requirements}

\subsubsection{Functional Requirements}

The following functional requirements are consolidated from the feature specification.

\begin{longtable}{p{0.18\linewidth} p{0.27\linewidth} p{0.43\linewidth}}
\caption{Functional requirements summary}\\
\toprule
Area & Requirement & Description \\
\midrule
\endhead
Guest Trial & Trial without login & Users can enter a guest dashboard, open one sample lesson, complete one sample exercise, and receive a soft registration prompt. \\
Guest Trial & Progress migration & Guest lesson view and exercise score are stored in local storage and merged into the account after registration. \\
Authentication & Email/password login & Users can register and log in with email and password. Password requires at least eight characters and one digit. \\
Authentication & Session management & JWT session persists for 30 days with logout, reset password, refresh behavior, and rate limiting. \\
Dashboard & Continue learning & The dashboard recommends the unfinished lesson, the next lesson, or the first lesson for new users. \\
Learning Path & Level and topic grouping & Lessons are organized by level and topic group with visible progress and lesson status. \\
Lesson Detail & Markdown lesson rendering & Lessons render headings, rich content, note callouts, warning callouts, audio examples, breadcrumbs, and CTAs. \\
Exercise & Three question types & Multiple choice, true/false, and fill-in-the-blank questions are supported. \\
Exercise & Feedback and retry & Users receive immediate feedback, final score, wrong-answer list, and can retry exercises. \\
Progress & Learning state & The system stores not-started, in-progress, and completed states per user and lesson. \\
Quick Note & Floating note panel & Users can create, edit, delete, and autosave notes from lessons, exercises, dictation, and shadowing screens. \\
Notes Page & Search and filter & Users can list notes, search by title/body, filter by lesson, and edit notes in a full-page view. \\
Dictation & Audio and transcript comparison & Users listen to short audio, type what they hear, and receive token-level diff and accuracy. \\
Bookmark & Saved items & Users can bookmark lessons or questions and view them in a saved items page. \\
Onboarding & Goal selection & Users can set a learning goal and optional exam date after registration. \\
Admin CMS & Content operations & Admin can create, preview, publish, unpublish, and manage lessons, exercises, dictation, shadowing, and learning path order. \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

\subsubsection{Representative User Stories}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
  \item As a guest, I want to try one lesson and one exercise before registering so that I can understand the product value.
  \item As a new learner, I want the dashboard to suggest my first lesson so that I do not need to choose randomly.
  \item As a returning learner, I want to continue the lesson I was studying so that I can resume quickly.
  \item As a learner, I want immediate exercise feedback so that I can correct mistakes while the context is fresh.
  \item As a learner, I want to take notes inside the lesson screen so that I do not need another tool while studying.
  \item As an exam-oriented learner, I want to set my exam date so that the dashboard can show a countdown.
  \item As an admin, I want to create and publish lessons without developer support so that content production can happen continuously.
\end{itemize}
