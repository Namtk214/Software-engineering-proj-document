\section{Software Requirements Specification}

\subsection{Introduction}

\subsubsection{Product Overview}

Francais.vn is a self-learning French platform for Vietnamese learners aged 15--30. The MVP delivers a structured learning path from absolute beginner (Khởi đầu) to CEFR A1, with lesson-based micro-exercises, listening practice, SRS vocabulary flashcards, and an admin CMS for continuous content operation.

The platform is built as a pnpm monorepo containing two Next.js applications: a learner-facing frontend (\texttt{apps/web}, port 3000) and a Payload CMS backend that serves both the admin panel and the REST/GraphQL API (\texttt{apps/cms}, port 3001), backed by PostgreSQL 16.

\paragraph{Elevator Pitch.}
Francais.vn helps Vietnamese learners follow a clear, structured path from zero to A1 French, practice through micro-exercises and listening activities, and track their own progress — all within a single web application that a non-technical founder can populate with content independently.

\paragraph{Vision.}
Become the leading French self-learning platform for Vietnamese learners by combining structured learning paths, evidence-based spaced repetition, personalised progress tracking, and exam-oriented preparation modules.

\subsubsection{Out of Scope}

Payment processing, AI grading or conversation, community features, native mobile applications, placement tests, full mock tests with timers, multi-admin role-based access control, push notifications, email drip campaigns, and the guest trial conversion funnel are outside the current MVP scope.

\subsection{User and System Requirements}

\subsubsection{Functional Requirements}

\begin{longtable}{p{0.16\linewidth} p{0.28\linewidth} p{0.44\linewidth}}
\caption{Functional requirements}\\
\toprule
Area & Requirement & Description \\
\midrule
\endhead
Authentication & Email / password registration & Learners register with email and password. The system sends a verification email via Resend. Unverified accounts cannot log in. \\
Authentication & Email verification & A tokenised link in the verification email activates the account. \\
Authentication & Google OAuth sign-in & Learners can authenticate with a Google account. The backend creates or finds the learner record and returns a signed JWT. \\
Authentication & JWT session & The JWT is stored in the browser's \texttt{localStorage} under the key \texttt{learner\_token} and sent as an \texttt{Authorization: JWT} header. Admin sessions use a separate \texttt{payload-token} cookie on port 3001. \\
Dashboard & Continue learning & The dashboard shows the most recent in-progress lesson, falling back to the most recently completed lesson, or the first lesson for new users. \\
Dashboard & Streak \& heatmap & The system computes a consecutive-day activity streak and an 84-day activity heatmap from LessonProgress timestamps at render time. \\
Dashboard & Spaced-repetition reminders & Completed lessons due for review (Ebbinghaus intervals: 1, 3, 7, 30 days) appear in a ``Review today'' section with forget-risk labels. \\
Learning Path & Level and unit grouping & Lessons are grouped under Units, which are grouped under Levels. Each lesson card shows its real progress tag: Mới, Đang học, or Hoàn thành. \\
Lesson Detail & Markdown rendering & Lessons render structured markdown content with headings, callout blocks, vocabulary lists, and linked media blocks. \\
Lesson Detail & Visit tracking & Opening a lesson automatically creates an in\_progress progress record (or updates \texttt{lastVisitedAt} if one already exists). \\
Exercise & Four question types & Multiple choice, true/false, fill-in-the-blank, and matching questions are rendered inline within the lesson page. \\
Exercise & Server-side grading & Answers are submitted to the web server, which fetches the question set from the CMS, grades each response, and returns per-question results. \\
Exercise & Scroll-based completion & For lessons without exercises, scrolling to 90\% of the page marks the lesson as completed. \\
Exercise & Progress update on completion & Submitting a graded exercise sets the LessonProgress state to \texttt{completed} and records \texttt{completedAt}. \\
Vocabulary & Dictionary and saved words & Learners can search French vocabulary and save words. Saved words are stored in \texttt{localStorage} with SRS scheduling metadata. \\
Vocabulary & SRS flashcard review & Saved words are surfaced in a flashcard session sorted by SRS due date. \\
Goal setting & Goal modal & Learners can set a learning goal, optional exam type, exam date, and daily minutes target. Goal data is stored in \texttt{localStorage}. \\
Admin CMS & Content hierarchy & Admins manage Levels, Units, Lessons, QuestionSets (with inline question blocks), Vocabularies, and Media through the Payload admin panel. \\
Admin CMS & Draft / publish workflow & Lessons and QuestionSets support draft and published states. Published content is visible to learners; drafts are not. \\
Admin CMS & Localisation & Localised fields (title, description) accept content in Vietnamese (default), French, and English. \\
Admin CMS & Auto-slug & Slugs for Units, Lessons, and QuestionSets are generated automatically from the title and guaranteed to be unique. \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

\subsubsection{Representative User Stories}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
  \item As a new learner, I want the dashboard to suggest my first lesson so that I do not need to choose randomly.
  \item As a returning learner, I want to continue the lesson I was studying so that I can resume without searching.
  \item As a learner, I want immediate per-question feedback after submitting an exercise so that I can correct mistakes while the context is fresh.
  \item As a learner, I want lessons I finished long ago to reappear as review reminders so that I do not forget what I learned.
  \item As a learner, I want to save French words and review them with flashcards so that vocabulary sticks over time.
  \item As a learner, I want to sign in with Google so that I do not need to manage a separate password.
  \item As an admin, I want to create and publish lessons from a web panel without developer help so that content production can happen independently and continuously.
\end{itemize}
